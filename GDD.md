# Game Design Document: DRP AI - The Supply Chain Frontier

## 1. Introduction
**Title:** DRP AI: The Supply Chain Frontier
**Genre:** Management Simulation / Strategy
**Platform:** PC / Web
**Tone:** Futuristic, Professional, High-Stakes

## 2. Concept
In a world where global supply chains are increasingly complex, you play as a Supply Chain Engineer. Your mission is to evolve traditional Distribution Requirements Planning (DRP) using cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence. The game focuses on moving away from tedious data entry toward strategic decision-making and providing real value to customers.

## 3. Key Pillars
*   **AI-Driven Automation:** Stop wasting time on "organizing data and structuring columns." The AI handles the grunt work.
*   **Predictive Analysis:** Discover "hidden sales" that aren't visible to the naked eye.
*   **Lead Time Mastery:** Manage supplier relationships and lead times through AI-reinforced agreements.
*   **Added Value:** Your role is to use AI insights to provide superior service to the end customer.

## 4. Gameplay Mechanics

### 4.1. The AI Data Core
*   **Automated Structuring:** Upon receiving raw data (shipments, inventory, sales), the player activates the "AI Core" which instantly cleans and organizes the data into usable DRP tables.
*   **Real-Time Dashboard:** A live feed showing integral analysis of product demand and search trends.

### 4.2. Demand Prediction (The "Hidden Sales" Mechanic)
*   **Deep Scan:** Players use AI tools to analyze market trends beyond historical sales.
*   **Insight Discovery:** AI highlights potential demand spikes that traditional methods miss, allowing players to stock up before a surge.

### 4.3. Supplier Management
*   **Lead Time Shield:** Negotiate and reinforce agreements with suppliers.
*   **Risk Mitigation:** AI predicts potential delays, allowing the player to proactively adjust agreements to maintain lead times.

### 4.4. Strategic Decisions
*   Since AI handles the data, the player spends "Action Points" on:
    *   Improving customer satisfaction.
    *   Developing new distribution routes.
    *   Upgrading AI algorithms for better precision.

## 5. Game Loop
1.  **Ingest:** Receive raw, messy supply chain data.
2.  **Process:** Use AI to organize and structure columns automatically.
3.  **Analyze:** Review real-time demand and identify "hidden sales" predictions.
4.  **Execute:** Reinforce supplier agreements and allocate inventory.
5.  **Deliver:** Provide added value to customers and receive a performance rating.

## 6. Visual Style
*   Sleek, minimalist UI with data visualizations.
*   Cybernetic aesthetic representing the connection between human engineering and artificial intelligence.
